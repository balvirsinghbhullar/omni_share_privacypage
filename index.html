Privacy Policy for Omni Share

Last Updated: October 21, 2026

Welcome to Omni Share. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application to upload, save, and share files.

1. How Omni Share Works

Omni Share is designed to make cross-platform file sharing simple and secure. When you use our app:

  • You authenticate securely using Google Sign-In.
  • You select a file to upload.
  • The file is transferred directly to and saved within your personal Google Drive account.
  • We generate a direct, shareable download link and a QR code based on that file's location in your Google Drive.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we request access to the following information:

  • Google Profile Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information (such as your name, email address, and profile picture) to identify your account and personalize your experience.
  • Google Drive Files: We request permissions to upload, create, and manage files in your Google Drive so the app can function. We only access the specific files you choose to upload through Omni Share.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly to operate the Omni Share application. Specifically:

  • To authenticate your identity via Google securely.
  • To upload your chosen files directly to your own Google Drive.
  • To generate and provide you with a direct download link and QR code for your uploaded files.
  • To improve the performance and user experience of the app.

We do NOT use your data for advertising, and we do NOT sell your data to any third parties.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Omni Share does not store your files on our own servers. All files uploaded through the app are stored directly in your personal Google Drive account. Consequently, the retention, security, and deletion of your files are governed by Google's privacy and security policies. You retain full control over your files and can delete them from your Google Drive at any time.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Omni Share's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We only use Google Drive scopes to read, write, and modify files you explicitly choose to upload via Omni Share.
  • We do not transfer your data to third parties unless necessary to provide our core features, comply with the law, or as part of a merger/acquisition.
  • We do not use or extract your data for serving advertisements.

6. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Authentication is handled entirely through Google's secure OAuth 2.0 protocol. This means Omni Share never sees or stores your Google account password. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Third-Party Links

Our application generates links for sharing files. If you share these links with others, the recipients will have access to download those specific files. Please be mindful of whom you share your download links and QR codes with.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
